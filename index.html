THE DAILY VECTOR

1. Click and drag a friendly (✈) to steer it. Friendly planes will continuously spawn: correct their flight paths; keep them clear of enemies and one another.
Drag wayward planes back onto the flight path to earn points. The sooner you redirect them, the more you score.

2. Click an enemy diamond, confirm, then press and hold for 2 seconds to strike it for points.

3. Press and hold a red ping for 0.5 seconds to identify it and earn points. Each ping expires after 3 seconds.

Failure to identify a ping before it expires or collision of entities means game over. Your daily rank is your CER = (Points / Time) × Difficulty.
